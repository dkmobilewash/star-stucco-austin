## Descriptions

**Short Description (150 chars):**
Star Stucco of Austin is Central Texas's trusted stucco contractor. Residential & commercial stucco, EIFS, plaster, and stone veneer. 9,000+ projects.

**Long Description (750 chars):**
Star Stucco of Austin is Central Texas's premier stucco and plaster contractor, serving the greater Austin area with over 9,000 completed projects. We specialize in residential stucco installation, commercial stucco systems, EIFS (Exterior Insulation and Finish Systems), interior plaster finishing, and thin stone veneer. Our experienced team handles everything from new construction stucco to stucco repair, crack remediation, and full exterior renovations. We serve Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Westlake, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Kyle, and surrounding Central Texas counties including Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, Burnet, and Blanco counties. Contact us for a free estimate.
